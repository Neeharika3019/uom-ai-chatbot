CATEGORY: Student Support Services

TITLE: University of Mauritius - Student Support and Welfare Services

INFORMATION:

The University of Mauritius provides various services to support students in their academic, personal, health, social and financial needs.

The main student support services include:

1. Students' Union
2. First Aid Services
3. Student Counselling
4. Library
5. Sports and Games
6. Student Welfare and Financial Assistance
7. International Student Support

---

1. STUDENTS' UNION

---

The Students' Union is the official organisation of students at the University of Mauritius.

It represents students in matters concerning student life and promotes student welfare and social, cultural and educational activities.

The Students' Union also supports and recognises student clubs and societies.

3. STUDENT COUNSELLING

---

The University of Mauritius provides counselling support for students who experience difficulties that may affect their studies.

Students may seek support for issues including:

* Difficulties in studies
* Personal problems
* Trauma
* Conflicts between peers
* Other difficulties affecting their studies

Students requiring counselling support can be referred through the Admissions and Student Records Office (ASRO).

Depending on the individual situation, students may be referred to appropriate professionals such as psychologists, family counsellors or psychiatrists.

Confidentiality is taken into consideration when dealing with student counselling cases.

6. STUDENT WELFARE AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

---

The University of Mauritius has a Student Welfare Office that provides support related to student welfare and financial assistance.

The University also provides financial assistance schemes for eligible students.

The UoM Students Financial Assistance Fund is available to eligible full-time, non-tuition-fee-paying undergraduate students.

Students applying for financial assistance must follow the application procedure and provide the required supporting documents.
