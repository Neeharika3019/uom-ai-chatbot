CATEGORY: Admission and Programme Structure

TITLE: University of Mauritius - Admission, Programme Structure and Credit System

INFORMATION:

1. ADMISSION

Applicants must follow the University's prescribed application procedure and submit the required information and documents within the specified deadline.

Applicants must satisfy the University's general entry requirements as well as the specific requirements for the programme to which they are applying.

The University may apply programme-specific selection criteria.

The University regulations cover:

* Application procedures
* Selection of students
* Admission of students
* General entry requirements
* Programme requirements
* Special admission cases
* Mature students
* Admission restrictions
* Auditeur Libre enrolment

2. PROGRAMME STRUCTURE

The University regulations define:

* Academic year
* Programme of study
* Modules
* Credit system
* Credit equivalence
* Number of credits and contact hours
* Programme duration
* Types of modules
* Mode of delivery
* Industrial placement
* Practical training
* Practicum
* Clinical placement
* Professional placement
* Equivalence between UoM credits, ECTS, UK and US credits

3. TYPES OF MODULES

Modules may include:

* Core modules
* Elective/optional modules
* Self-study modules
* Independent study
* Audit modules

4. PROGRAMME CREDITS

The 2026/2027 regulations provide different credit ranges according to the type of qualification.

Examples include:

3-year undergraduate degree:
96-106 UoM credits under the UoM credit system.

3½-year undergraduate degree:
105-112 UoM credits under the UoM credit system.

4-year undergraduate degree:
120-130 UoM credits under the UoM credit system.

Undergraduate Top-Up degree:
36-54 UoM credits.

Postgraduate Certificate:
12-18 UoM credits.

Postgraduate Diploma:
24-30 UoM credits.

Master's Award:
36-42 UoM credits.

Note:

The University is implementing the National Credit Value and Transfer System (NCVTS) from Academic Year 2026/2027. Programme catalogues and regulations are being revised accordingly.

5. PROGRAMME DURATION

The regulations specify normal and maximum durations for different programme types.

For example:

Undergraduate 3-year degree:
Normal full-time duration: 3 years.

Undergraduate 3½-year degree:
Normal full-time duration: 3½ years.

Undergraduate 4-year degree:
Normal full-time duration: 4 years.

Undergraduate Top-Up degree:
Normal full-time duration: 1 year.

Other programme durations depend on the programme type and mode of study.

Students should consult their specific Programme Structure/Catalogue for programme-specific requirements.
