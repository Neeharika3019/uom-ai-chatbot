ATTENDANCE:

Students are expected to attend the lectures, tutorials and other forms of instruction prescribed by their programme.

Attendance is monitored by the University and may be taken into consideration by the Board of Examiners and relevant University authorities.

Students should therefore check the attendance requirements applicable to their specific programme/module.

STUDENT CONDUCT:

Students are expected to conduct themselves in a manner consistent with the objectives, efficiency and well-being of the University as an academic community.

Breaches of University discipline may result in disciplinary action under the University's Code of Discipline.

UNIVERSITY NOTICES:

Official notices displayed on University notice boards, official Faculty/Centre notice boards, posted on the University website and/or sent to students' official University email addresses are considered to be known to students.

WITHDRAWAL:

A student who wishes to completely withdraw from the University must follow the prescribed withdrawal procedure and submit the appropriate withdrawal form.
