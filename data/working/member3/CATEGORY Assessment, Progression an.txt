CATEGORY: Assessment, Progression and Awards

TITLE: University of Mauritius - Assessment, Progression, Resits and Awards

INFORMATION:

The University regulations cover assessment, continuous assessment, written examinations, GPA, CPA, prerequisites, Grade N, retakes, special retakes, resits, progression, repeating a year, termination of registration, classification of awards and conferment of qualifications.

1. ASSESSMENT

Students are assessed according to the requirements of their modules and programme.

Assessment may include:

* Written examinations
* Continuous assessment
* Laboratory work
* Seminars
* Assignments
* Class tests

The exact assessment structure for a module should be checked in the relevant Module Specification Sheet or Module Catalogue.

2. CONTINUOUS ASSESSMENT

Continuous assessment may include laboratory work, seminars, assignments and class tests.

The normal weighting of continuous assessment to written examination is:

Undergraduate programmes:
20% to 40% continuous assessment, unless otherwise specified.

Postgraduate programmes:
30% to 40% continuous assessment, unless otherwise specified.

Specific programme/module requirements may differ.

3. GPA AND CPA

The University uses Grade Point Average (GPA), Cumulative Point Average (CPA) and Level/Year Point Average calculations for academic assessment and progression.

4. PREREQUISITES

Some modules may have:

* Pre-requisites (PR)
* Pre-requirements (PQ)
* Minimum requirements (MR)

Students must satisfy applicable requirements before registering for certain modules.

5. GRADE N

Grade N is used in circumstances specified by the University's assessment regulations, including certain cases involving absence due to approved circumstances.

6. RETAKES AND RESITS

The regulations provide for:

* Retake modules
* Special retake examinations
* Resit examinations
* Re-submission of dissertations
* Assessment of phased-out modules

7. PROGRESSION

Progression from one year/level to another is governed by the programme's applicable yearly or semester regulations.

For programmes under yearly regulations, the 2025/2026 regulations state that a student normally needs a CPA of at least 40, or 50 for some programmes, unless otherwise specified in the Programme Structure.

Students must also satisfy applicable prerequisite, pre-requirement or minimum requirement conditions.

Students should always consult the latest applicable programme regulations because programme-specific requirements may differ.

8. REPEATING A YEAR

The regulations provide conditions under which students may repeat a year.

Repeating is subject to the University's academic regulations and the maximum duration permitted for the programme.

9. TERMINATION OF REGISTRATION

The University regulations specify circumstances under which a student's registration may be terminated.

10. CLASSIFICATION OF AWARDS

The University regulations specify how final awards are classified according to the applicable programme and academic results.
