CATEGORY: University Regulations

TITLE: University of Mauritius - Regulations 2026/2027 Overview

INFORMATION:

The University of Mauritius publishes regulations governing students, programmes, registration, assessment, examinations, academic integrity, final year projects, student welfare, fees and other academic matters.

The current University of Mauritius regulations page provides a 2026-2027 set of regulations.

IMPORTANT:

The 2026-2027 regulations are currently marked "Under Review" by the University of Mauritius. Regulations may therefore be subject to change.

The University of Mauritius also states that the National Credit Value and Transfer System (NCVTS) is applicable to all cohorts from Academic Year 2026/2027. Related programme information and regulations are being revised to align with this system.
