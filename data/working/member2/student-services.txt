SECTION 1: UoM LIBRARY SERVICES & REGULATIONS
================================================================================

1.1 Library Opening Hours (Term Time): Weekdays (Monday to Friday): 08:00–17:00, Saturdays: 08:00–13:00.
1.2 Library Opening Hours (Vacation Period): Weekdays (Monday to Friday): 08:00–16:00, Saturdays: 08:00–12:00 (Noon).

1.3 Library Membership & Borrowing Allowances: All borrowers must present a valid UoM Student/Staff ID card to borrow books.
- Academic Staff: Up to 6 books (including 2 from the Reserve Section)
- MPhil / PhD Students: Up to 5 books (including 1 from the Reserve Section)
- Undergraduate Students: Up to 3 books (including 1 from the Reserve Section)
- Law Students: Up to 3 books (including 1 from the Reserve Section)
- Non-Academic Staff: Up to 1 book
Reference Link: https://www.uom.ac.mu/index.php/library/library-regulations/membership
