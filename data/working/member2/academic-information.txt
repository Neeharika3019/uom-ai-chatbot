1. Semester 1 Calendar and Major Activities (2026)

Lectures Start: Semester 1 lectures start for all students on Monday 03 August 2026[1][2].

Exemptions and Self-Study: Applications for Exemption(s) and Self-Study Module(s) open on Monday 03 August 2026[2].

Module Registration and Changes: The deadline for Exemption applications, Self-Study applications, Change of Module(s), Withdrawal from Module(s), and End of Module Registration is Friday 21 August 2026[1].

Floating Week: Floating Week for assessments, lectures, tutorials, and catch-up lectures takes place during Week 6 from Monday 07 September 2026 to Saturday 12 September 2026[3].

Late Registration: The last date for Late Module Registration or De-registration upon payment of a penalty fee is Friday 25 September 2026[4].

Student Feedback: Online Student Feedback Questionnaires for Semester 1 and yearly modules open on Monday 28 September 2026 and close on Friday 09 October 2026[4][5].

Interruption of Studies: The last date to apply for Interruption of Studies on non-emergency grounds is Friday 09 October 2026[4].

Completion of Teaching: Completion of all lectures, practicals, and tutorials for Semester 1 occurs on Saturday 17 October 2026[5].

Revision Weeks: Revision Weeks for Semester 1 take place from Monday 19 October 2026 to Saturday 31 October 2026[5][6].

Examinations and Semester End: Semester 1 examinations start on Tuesday 03 November 2026, and examinations end and Semester 1 officially concludes on Saturday 28 November 2026[5].
