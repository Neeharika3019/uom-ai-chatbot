CATEGORY: Fees, Student Support and Other Regulations

TITLE: University of Mauritius - Fees, Welfare, Scholarships and Other Student Regulations

INFORMATION:

The University regulations also cover student welfare, financial assistance, scholarships, fees, international students, library regulations, students with disabilities, the Students' Union and academic dress.

1. FEES

The University regulations cover:

* General fees
* Tuition fees
* Students' Welfare Fund
* Laboratory fees
* Clinical fees
* Placement fees
* Dissertation fees
* Refund of fees
* Late module registration fees
* Module exemption fees
* Re-examination fees
* Re-registration fees
* Remarking/review fees
* Appeal fees
* Transcript and certificate charges
* Student ID card charges
* Graduation fees
* Other applicable charges

The University reserves the right to review fees and other charges for a given academic year.

2. STUDENT WELFARE

The regulations include provisions relating to student welfare and financial support.

Students should consult the current official University information for the latest eligibility requirements, amounts and application deadlines.

3. FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE AND SCHOLARSHIPS

The University provides information concerning:

* Assistance to needy students
* Scholarships
* Prizes
* Medals

4. INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS

The regulations cover matters including:

* Application procedures
* General entry requirements
* International student fees
* Short-term study fees
* Dissertation fees
* Student Welfare Office
* Visa/residence permit
* Accommodation
* Other requirements for international students

5. STUDENT FACILITIES AND WELFARE

The regulations cover student facilities including:

* Students' Union
* First Aid
* Library
* Sports and Games
* Food outlets
* Banks
* Parking
* Student Counselling Unit
* Photocopy and printing
* Confucius Institute
* Souvenir items

6. LIBRARY

The University has regulations concerning library membership and the use of library facilities.

7. STUDENTS WITH DISABILITIES

The regulations cover:

* Definition of disability
* Admission of students with disabilities
* Facilities
* Relevant regulations
* Certification of disability

8. STUDENT ACADEMIC PROGRESS AND COMPLAINTS

The University has a Student Academic Progress and Complaints Committee.

The regulations define the committee's terms of reference, composition and operational guidelines.

9. STUDENTS' UNION

The University regulations include provisions relating to the Students' Union.

10. ACADEMIC DRESS

The University regulations contain requirements concerning academic dress.
