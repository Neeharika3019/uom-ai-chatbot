CATEGORY: Final Year Project

TITLE: University of Mauritius - Final Year Project and Dissertation Regulations

INFORMATION:

The University of Mauritius has specific regulations governing final year projects and dissertations.

The regulations cover:

1. Project/dissertation introduction
2. Identification and allocation of projects
3. Undertaking the project
4. Dissertation requirements
5. Referencing
6. Project/dissertation assessment
7. Submission and re-submission
8. Review of projects/dissertations
9. Project declaration forms
10. Group project declaration and submission forms
11. Project proposal/synopsis
12. Progress log
13. Non-disclosure agreements where applicable

PROJECT ALLOCATION:

Final year projects/dissertations are allocated according to the University's prescribed procedures.

Students must comply with the requirements set by their Faculty/Centre and programme.

PROJECT WORK:

Students are expected to conduct their project according to the requirements of the University, Faculty/Centre, programme and supervisor.

REFERENCING:

Students must follow the University of Mauritius referencing requirements.

The University provides guidance on the Harvard referencing system and other accepted referencing requirements.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY:

Students must ensure that project/dissertation work is their own.

Sources, quotations, ideas and other contributions must be properly acknowledged.

The use of Artificial Intelligence must be appropriately attributed and cited/referenced where applicable.

GROUP PROJECTS:

For group projects/dissertations, students must complete the relevant group project declaration and submission forms.

The declaration confirms that the group has understood and complied with the University's requirements relating to plagiarism, fabrication/falsification and referencing.

TURNITIN:

Final project/dissertation submissions require compliance with the University's Turnitin submission requirements.

Failure to submit through the required Turnitin platform may result in the project/dissertation being treated as unreceivable.

ASSESSMENT:

Final year projects/dissertations are assessed according to the University's prescribed procedures.

Assessment may involve the written dissertation and may also include a viva-voce and/or poster presentation, depending on the applicable requirements.

Projects may be assessed by the supervisor and another examiner/assessor according to the University's regulations.

LATE SUBMISSION:

The University has penalties and procedures for late submission of projects/dissertations.

Students with genuine circumstances beyond their control may be required to provide evidence within the specified period for consideration by the relevant Faculty/Centre authorities.

PROJECT DECLARATION:

Students are required to declare that:

* They have understood the University's plagiarism and fabrication/falsification requirements.
* They have properly referenced sources.
* Contributions and quotations from others have been acknowledged.
* Use of Artificial Intelligence has been appropriately attributed and referenced.
* They have not allowed others to copy their work.
