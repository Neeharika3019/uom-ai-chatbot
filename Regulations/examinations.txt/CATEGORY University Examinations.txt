CATEGORY: University Examinations

TITLE: University of Mauritius - Examination Regulations

INFORMATION:

The University has regulations governing admission to examinations, examination procedures, absence from examinations, examiners, publication of results, examination irregularities, cheating and review of examination scripts.

1. ADMISSION TO EXAMINATIONS

Students must satisfy the University's requirements for admission to examinations.

Students should check official examination information and notices issued by the University.

2. CONDUCT DURING EXAMINATIONS

Students must comply with examination regulations and instructions from examination staff and invigilators.

Unauthorised devices are prohibited.

A student found using an unauthorised device during an examination may be reported for breach of examination regulations and the device may be confiscated.

3. EXAMINATION MATERIALS

Students must follow instructions concerning examination answer books and supplementary sheets.

Answer books and supplementary sheets must not be removed from the examination room after examinations.

4. IMPERSONATION

Impersonation during examinations is treated as a serious examination offence.

A student found guilty of impersonation during examinations will be expelled from the University.

5. ABSENCE FROM EXAMINATIONS

If a student is absent from an examination/class test without compelling reasons, the student is deemed to have failed the examination/class test.

If absence is due to ill health, the student must submit a valid Medical Certificate from a registered public/private medical practitioner to the Dean's/Director's Office within 3 working days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and Public Holidays.

Where the medical absence requirements are satisfied, Grade N may be awarded and the student may be allowed to retake the module when next offered.

Absence due to other extenuating circumstances may be considered by the appropriate University authorities.

6. EXAMINATION IRREGULARITIES

Examination irregularities may be referred to the Discipline Committee (Examination & Plagiarism).

The student will be notified of the charges.

A student may have the right to plead guilty and may have the right to appeal, subject to the University's procedures and deadlines.

7. APPEALS

The current examination regulations provide an appeal process for disciplinary decisions arising from examination irregularities.

Students must follow the prescribed procedure and applicable deadlines.

8. REVIEW OF EXAMINATION SCRIPTS

The regulations provide procedures for students who wish to request a review of examination scripts.
