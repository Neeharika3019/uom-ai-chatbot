2\. Library Membership and Borrowing Limits

All borrowers must present a valid UoM Student/Staff ID card to borrow books[5]:

Academic Staff: 6 books (including 2 from the Reserve Section)[5].
MPhil / PhD Students: 5 books (including 1 from the Reserve Section)[5]
Undergraduate and Law Students: 3 books (including 1 from the Reserve Section)[5].
Non-Academic Staff: 1 book[5].
Official Regulations URL: `https://www.uom.ac.mu/index.php/library/library-regulations/membership`[5].
