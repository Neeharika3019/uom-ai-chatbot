CATEGORY: Faculties and Departments

TITLE: University of Mauritius - Faculties and Departments

INFORMATION:

The University of Mauritius has seven main faculties:

1. Faculty of Agriculture

2. Faculty of Engineering

3. Faculty of Information, Communication and Digital Technologies

4. Faculty of Law & Management

5. Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences

6. Faculty of Science

7. Faculty of Social Sciences & Humanities

---

1. FACULTY OF AGRICULTURE

---

The Faculty of Agriculture has two departments:

1. Department of Agricultural Production & Systems (APS)

2. Department of Agricultural & Food Science (AFS)

The Department of Agricultural Production & Systems is involved in teaching, research and consultancy related to agricultural production and systems.

The Department of Agricultural & Food Science carries out teaching, research, consultancy and outreach in areas including biotechnology and microbiology, food science and technology, aquaculture, crop protection, and agricultural and soil chemistry.

---

2. FACULTY OF ENGINEERING

---

The Faculty of Engineering has five departments:

1. Department of Chemical and Environmental Engineering

2. Department of Civil Engineering

3. Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering

4. Department of Applied Sustainability and Enterprise Development

5. Department of Mechanical and Production Engineering

The Faculty offers engineering education and research in areas including chemical engineering, environmental engineering, civil engineering, electrical and electronic engineering, sustainability, enterprise development, mechanical engineering and production engineering.

---

3. FACULTY OF INFORMATION, COMMUNICATION AND DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES

---

The Faculty of Information, Communication and Digital Technologies has three departments:

1. Department of Digital Technologies (DT)

2. Department of Information and Communication Technologies (ICT)

3. Department of Software and Information Systems (SIS)

The Department of Digital Technologies covers areas including mobile and smart technologies, digital technologies, multimedia, computer graphics, image processing, human-computer interaction and related technologies.

The Department of Information and Communication Technologies provides education and training in ICT-related areas. It offers programmes including BSc (Hons) Computer Science and BSc (Hons) Cyber Security.

The Department of Software and Information Systems covers areas including business systems, enterprise applications, software development, information management, knowledge management, big data, information systems, data science and artificial intelligence.

---

4. FACULTY OF LAW & MANAGEMENT

---

The Faculty of Law & Management has three departments:

1. Department of Finance & Accounting

2. Department of Law

3. Department of Management

The Department of Finance & Accounting covers areas related to accounting, finance and financial technology.

The Department of Law provides legal education and offers programmes in areas including law and criminal justice.

The Department of Management covers areas including management, human resources, digital marketing, international business, entrepreneurship and tourism.

---

5. FACULTY OF MEDICINE AND HEALTH SCIENCES

---

The Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences has two departments:

1. Department of Medicine

2. Department of Health Sciences

The Department of Medicine provides education and training in medicine and related areas.

The Department of Health Sciences covers allied health areas including nutritional sciences, occupational therapy, physiotherapy, pharmacy, sanitary sciences, biomedical sciences, medical imaging technology, nursing and sports sciences.

The Faculty also hosts an Ayurveda Chair established in collaboration with the Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India.

---

6. FACULTY OF SCIENCE

---

The Faculty of Science has four departments:

1. Department of Biological Sciences and Ocean Studies

2. Department of Chemistry

3. Department of Mathematics

4. Department of Physics

The Faculty of Science provides education and research in a range of scientific disciplines including biological sciences, ocean studies, chemistry, mathematics and physics.

---

7. FACULTY OF SOCIAL SCIENCES & HUMANITIES

---

The Faculty of Social Sciences & Humanities has five departments:

1. Department of Economics and Statistics

2. Department of English Studies

3. Department of French Studies

4. Department of History and Political Science

5. Department of Social Studies

The Faculty covers a wide range of areas including language studies, literature, cultural studies, history, anthropology, heritage studies, international relations, sociology, gender studies, social work, psychology, social development, political science, human rights, economics, statistics, public policy, philosophy and related fields.
